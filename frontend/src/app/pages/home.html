How to play
  • Roll a 6 to bring a piece out of your base.
  • Move clockwise around the board and up your coloured lane to the centre.
  • Land on an opponent to knock them back to base, except on ★ and start squares, which are safe.
  • A 6, a knockout, or getting a piece home gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row and you lose your turn.
  • You need the exact number to reach home. First to get all four pieces home wins!
