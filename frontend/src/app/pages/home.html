How to play
  • Pick a hero. Each has spells: the Samurai slashes the tiles ahead, the Grand Wizard zaps and blinks.
  • Roll a 6 to bring a hero out of your camp, then race along the trail to the temple.
  • Land on an enemy to smash them back to camp. Start tiles, ✦ star tiles and your home path are safe.
  • Gain mana every turn, on a roll of 1, at shrines and when you get knocked out. Spend it on one spell per turn.
  • A 6, a knockout or reaching the temple gives another roll. First to bring every hero home wins.
